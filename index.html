My collection

Riot doesn't let outside websites read your account inventory, so tick what you own here. Turn on Owned only to roll from your collection. Unowned guns roll as the standard skin.

Sign in with Google or Discord to sync your collection and recent challenges across devices. This app never asks for your Riot password or reads Riot inventory.
